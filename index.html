THE LOOPING STREET
Neon rain falls in slow motion. Cars glide through the intersection on a perfect loop: same path, same timing. Pedestrians repeat the same gestures like corrupted holograms. Digital billboards flicker, stuck on the same three frames.

You are the only thing in sharp focus. Everything else looks like motion-blur long-exposure photography.
